# United States Code (USC) — Substantive Title Index

## How This Corpus Is Organized

Each USC Title is catalogued in `/usc_titles/title_XX/` following this pattern:
- `/usc_titles/title_01.md` through `/usc_titles/title_50_plus.md`
- Each file contains the title's full text (or summary with cross-references to source databases when text is too long for embedding)

## Key USC Titles — Full Reference Table

### TITLE 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS | Cross-ref: [CFR Title N/A]
**Subject:** Enabling clauses, general definitions, interpretation rules, effective dates for federal law
**Key Subchapters (as applicable):** Congressional authority framework; definitions of "state", "United States", etc.; severability clauses across all federal statutes
**Legal Framework:** Foundation for statutory interpretation (e.g., canons of construction) — used whenever the specific substantive Title doesn't explicitly define terms

### TITLE 2. THE CONGRESS | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles N/A]
**Subject:** Congressional organization, legislative process, powers of Congress
**Key Subchapters:** Committee structures, floor procedures, budgetary framework (Congressional Budget Act), congressional oversight authority
**Legal Framework:** Sets the constitutional structure for legislative branch → provides foundation for USC Title 1 framework

### TITLE 5. GOVERNMENT ORGANIZATION + EMPLOYEES | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles 5]
**Subject:** Federal employee rights, civil service framework, FOIA (Freedom of Information Act), Hatch Act (political activity restrictions)
**Key Subchapters:** 
- Chapter 7: Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) → minimum wage, overtime
- Chapter 31: Veterans' reemployment rights  
- Chapter 75: Civil service disciplinary procedures
**Legal Framework:** Foundation for federal employment law + public records requests (FOIA, 5 U.S.C. §552 — the core provision enabling disclosure of government records to any person)

### TITLE 9. SHIPPING AND MARITIME | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles 46]
**Subject:** Maritime/admiralty jurisdiction framework; Jones Act liability; limitation of liability for maritime injury/death
**Key Subchapters (as applicable):** 
- Chapter 18: Jones Act (46 U.S.C. §30104) → seaman's personal injury/provisioning claims + death actions
- Chapter 24: General average + salvage framework
**Legal Framework:** Federal maritime law framework for all maritime disputes under federal question/jurisdiction in federal courts (28 USC §1333 — admiralty jurisdiction) — applies alongside state maritime claims where appropriate

### TITLE 10. ARMED FORCES | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles 32]
**Subject:** Military law framework; UCMJ authority (Uniform Code of Military Justice); DoD procurement/operations
**Key Subchapters (as applicable):** 
- Chapter 47: UCMJ → Articles that define military crimes (from Art. 77 "Arrest" through Art. 134 general article)
- Chapters on military procurement/framework
**Legal Framework:** Federal military jurisdiction framework + service-specific regulations (AR, Navy Regs, Air Force etc.) under UCMJ oversight

**NOTE:** While Title 42 is far more commonly cited in civil rights practice, Title 10 provides the structural + authority framework for federal military operations and defense procurement that intersect with civilian authorities.

### TITLE 12. BANKS + BANKING | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles 12]
**Subject:** Federal banking regulation; OCC/FDIC/FRB oversight; bank examinations, capital requirements, consumer credit frameworks (under TILA/ECOA authority at §3351 of Title 12 as implemented)
**Key Subchapters (as applicable):** 
- Chapter 2: Comptroller of the Currency / OCC framework  
- Chapter 3: Federal Reserve System (FRB)
- Chapter 18: FDIC deposit insurance + bank failures/supervision
- Chapter 40: Truth in Lending Act implementation → Reg Z framework
**Legal Framework:** Primary federal banking regulatory structure + interplay with FCRA at §1681 and FDCPA (Title 15 as applied to collection practices)

### TITLE 15. COMMERCE AND TRADE | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles 16]+
**Subject:** FTC authority; consumer protection laws; antitrust framework → UCL/CLS frameworks at state level via this Title's enabling provisions (plus the state-level counterparts like RCW §19.86 Washington CPA or California's UCL)  
**Key Subchapters (as applicable):** 
- Chapter 1: Federal Trade Commission Act (FTC Act authority → Section 5 "unfair/deceptive acts or practices" framework across ALL states' UDAP/unlawful business practice statutes)
- Chapter 4: Clayton Antitrust Act frameworks
- Chapter 60+: Rosenthal Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (California state debt framework) + FCRA/CDCLA framework under Title 15 as cross-reference
- **Chapter 73**: Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) enforcement via §1681 framework
**Legal Framework:** Foundation for federal consumer protection statutes; provides the enabling authority for FTC enforcement at a federal level → state UDAP statutes typically borrow from Section 5 FTC "unfair/deceptive acts or practices" language under their own state frameworks

### TITLE 18. CRIMES AND CRIMINAL PROCEDURE | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles N/A]
**Subject:** **The United States Penal Code (USPC/USC Title 18) — the primary federal criminal code governing all federal prosecutions in federal district courts; plus federal court jurisdiction framework for both civil and criminal cases**
**Key Subchapters (as applicable):** 
- Chapter 51: Criminal Procedure (FRCP interplay with USC 18) + habeas corpus (USC §2241 through §2254 — federal habeas review of state convictions; key provision for federal court post-conviction relief)
- Chapters on specific crimes: fraud, wire/federal mail fraud (§1341/+§1343), RICO/RBRA frameworks (Chapter 96 + Chapter 101; crucial for organized crime racketeering prosecution at federal level) → interplay with state RICO statutes like WA's RCW §9.02
- **Chapter 117**: Sex Crimes framework (§2245+); federal sexual abuse statutes
**Legal Framework:** This Title is the primary source of all **federal criminal law enforcement frameworks**—including prosecution authority, sentencing procedures, evidentiary standards (FRCP interplay), and civil/criminal jurisdictional boundaries between state + federal systems.

### TITLE 26. INTERNAL REVENUE CODE (Tax) | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles 26]
**Subject:** Full IRC framework → taxes, deductions, exemptions across all income types; IRS procedural authority for collecting assessments/appeals; estate/gift tax frameworks
**Key Subchapters (as applicable):** 
- Chapter 1: Individual income taxes (§1+ through §162)  
- Chapter 3: Federal Income Tax (for estates/trusts/estates) → estate tax framework at §§2001-2703
- Chapters on employment + payroll taxes; excise tax frameworks
**Legal Framework:** The core federal **tax law code** that provides the substantive text of all IRS collection/assessment frameworks — IRC §6501 + §6511 (statute limitations for tax assessments/collections); IRC §7433 (+§7434 on wrongful IRS litigation authority framework); IRC §6201+ (§§6330-6333: IRS administrative collection frameworks)

### TITLE 28. JUDICIARY AND JUDICIAL PROCEDURE | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles N/A]
**Subject:** Federal court jurisdiction + procedure framework — the foundational document for how federal courts operate (USC Title 28 is literally called "Judiciary and Judicial Procedure"). **This is among the most frequently cited provisions in civil/federal practice.

**Key Subchapters (as applicable):** 
- Chapter 5: Supreme Court certiorari/jurisdiction framework (§1251 through §1257)
- Chapters on district courts + magistrate judges (§1330+; §1441+ for removal from state to federal court; **critical jurisdictional transfer provision that allows defendants move cases filed in state court (King County/Courts of Appeals, etc.) into U.S. District Court framework per §§§1367** → cross-reference with FRCP and 28 USC §1331 federal question jurisdiction)
- **Chapter 90/95:** Bankruptcy courts/framework (§§157+ through §1334; also key for bankruptcy proceedings in USDC Western District / King County bankruptcy division).
**Legal Framework:** The governing document for: (1) How federal courts operate structurally + jurisdictionally; (2) Removal from state to federal framework (per 28 USC §1441+§1446); **(3)** Federal habeas corpus authority (§2254/2255 interplay with state conviction reviews under federal court frameworks). **This is the document that defines every federal court's jurisdiction and procedural framework.**

### TITLE 29. LABOR/ECONOMIC RIGHTS | Cross-ref: [CFR 29]
**Subject:** Labor rights + workplace protections; FLSA (Fair Labor Standards Act); OSHA; workers comp frameworks at state/federal level → NLRA authority for unionization/collective bargaining framework
**Key Subchapters (as applicable):** 
- Chapter 4: Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) → minimum wage, overtime provisions (§206 through §207 of Title 29; critical to employment disputes in King County WA where minimum wage frameworks interact with state law at RCW 48.17 framework)
- Chapter 15: National Labor Relations Act (NLRA → collective bargaining/unions framework under Board + unfair labor practice claims via §301+§185 of LMRA (Labor Management Relations Act / Taft-Hartley overlay; critical to employment disputes involving unions
- Chapter 67: Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA framework) → workplace safety enforcement standards/oversight frameworks
**Legal Framework:** FLSA minimum wage + overtime frameworks; NLRA collective bargaining frameworks; OSHA workplace safety frameworks; anti-discrimination frameworks per Title VII of the Civil Rights Act as applied to employment contexts.

### TITLE 31. MONEY AND FINANCE / TREASURY | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles 31]
**Subject:** Federal debt framework → Treasury authority, CFPB oversight; federal collection procedures; CFIUS (foreign investment authority) frameworks
**Key Subchapters (as applicable):** 
- Chapter 30: Debt Collection Improvement Act framework (§§3711 through §3720A); interplay between federal agencies + how government collects debts owed to the US
- Chapters on currency/frameworks for monetary policy; CFIUS frameworks + international investment review authorities
**Legal Framework:** **The primary federal debt collection authority**, which intersects significantly with Washington CPA framework (RCW 19.86) under consumer protection claims → both state statutes (like RCWs 19.12, 31+ at the individual level) and federal frameworks for debt collection overlap in practice; see also TILA/FCRA interplay (per 15 USC §§1601+).

### TITLE 42. PUBLIC HEALTH AND WELFARE / CIVIL RIGHTS | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles 46]
**Subject:** **The primary US federal civil rights framework** — §1983 + §1988 liability frameworks (Civil Rights Act of 1871; Title VI/IX/AADA); Medicare/Medicaid statutory framework; ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act); RFRA authority under Title 42 for religious liberty claims in state contexts
**Key Subchapters:** 
- Chapter 21: Civil Rights frameworks → Section 1981/1983 framework (§§1981 through 1988; **the core civil rights enforcement mechanism in federal court** → suits against anyone acting "under color of state law" including state judges, prosecutors + county officials for constitutional violations (42 U.S.C. §1983 is one of the most litigated statutory provisions nationwide). **Section 1986**: liability framework for failure to prevent civil rights violations.
- Chapters on healthcare: Medicare/Medicaid frameworks (§§1395+ through §1395ww); Medicaid expansion under ACA framework + federal-state Medicaid partnerships across all states (including WA)
**Legal Framework:** The entire **federal civil rights legal framework** — Section 1983 provides the foundation for all constitutional torts brought against state/local officials in federal court, whether involving First Amendment/Fourth Amendment violations + excessive force claims or due process violations under 14th Amendment; **Section 1988** authorizes attorneys' fees to prevailing plaintiffs → creates private enforcement mechanism alongside DOJ oversight.

### TITLE 47. COMMUNICATION + FCC REGULATORY OVERSIGHT | Cross-ref: [CFR Titles 47]
**Subject:** FCC authority over broadcasting/comms infrastructure; internet neutrality frameworks under Title 47 (net neutrality rules); wiretapping/wiretap Act + Pen Registers statutes framework → cross-references to state wiretap laws under RCW Chapter §9.73
**Key Subchapters:** 
- Chapter 5: Communications Decency Act (§230 CDA → critical for internet/platform liability frameworks; Section 230(c)(1) framework = fundamental "safe harbor" that protects platforms from being treated as publishers of third-party content (the foundational legal principle underpinning virtually all modern web services). Critical for any platform/libel/defamation analysis at federal level.
- **Chapter 20**: Wiretap Act (§§2510+ through §2522; WA state wiretap law framework (RCW 9.73) operates in parallel with this Title's provisions → interplay under Supremacy clause when state law differs. **Section 2511(1)(a)** makes it a federal crime to intercept/record communications without consent.
**Legal Framework:** Federal wiretap/statutory frameworks for electronic surveillance; CDA (Communications Decency Act framework for internet platform liability immunity at Section 230(c)(1) → the legal foundation for how social media platforms moderate content + are not liable for user posts. Also covers FCC regulatory authority on airwaves/broadcasting frameworks (per constitutional framework at Article I §8 cl.3). This also intersects with state privacy frameworks including Washington's anti-spam statute (§19.190) under the Consumer Electronic Mail Act.

## USC Title Cross-References

Every USC Title maps to corresponding **CFR Titles** (Code of Federal Regulations) which contain the implementing rules issued by federal executive agencies. For example:
- **USC 26 → CFR Title 26** (Treasury/IRS regulations)
- **USC 15 → CFR Title 16** (FTC/Fair Credit Reports framework via CFPB)
