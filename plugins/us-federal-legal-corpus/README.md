# us-federal-legal-corpus

Reference corpus for broad U.S. federal legal research and drafting support.

## Contents

- `references/us_constitution/full_text.md` - U.S. Constitution, Articles I-VII and Amendments I-XXVII. The original constitutional text is sourced from the National Archives transcription of the Constitution; the amendments are sourced from the National Archives Bill of Rights and Amendments 11-27 pages.
- `references/usc_titles/index.md` - index of current United States Code titles, including reserved Title 53 and concise subject-matter summaries.
- `references/cfr_regulations/index.md` - index of Code of Federal Regulations titles with the principal agencies or regulatory systems appearing in each title.

## Source Notes

The U.S. Code title list is based on the Office of the Law Revision Counsel current-release download page. The CFR title names are based on the eCFR title API, with agency mappings summarized from CFR chapter organization and agency names used by eCFR and the Office of the Federal Register.

This corpus is a research aid, not legal advice. Always verify current law, effective dates, amendments, and agency rulemaking history before relying on a citation in a filing or client-facing document.
