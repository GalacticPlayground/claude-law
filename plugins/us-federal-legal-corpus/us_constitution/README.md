### Post-Bill of Rights Amendments (XI–XXVII)

#### XI – XXV (selected highlights)
- XII (Presidential election) / XIII (slavery abolition / involuntary servitude exception)  
- XIV (Equal Protection Clause — incorporated Bill of Rights; equal protection test framework: scrutiny tiers per *Craig v. Boren*, *Loving v. Virginia*)  
  - **14th §5**: "Congress shall have power to enforce … by appropriate legislation" → basis for Title VII, ADEA, ADA via Commerce-Clause-enforcement power 
- XV (Voting rights) / XIX (Women's suffrage) / XXIV (no poll tax) / XXVI (18-year-old vote)
- **Amendment 26 (1971)**: Lowered voting age from 21 to 18 → triggered by Vietnam-era arguments "old enough to fight, old enough to vote"  
- **XXVII (1992)**: Congressional compensation — the last ratified amendment after >200 years; prevents mid-session raises until AFTER next election

### Federalism & Supremacy Framework
The Tenth Amendment + Article VI's Supremacy Clause establish the federal-state division. The *Commerce Clause* (Art. I, §8, cl. 3) provides the substantive anchor for most federal regulation of state and private activity; the *Necessary and Proper Clause* (Art. I, §8, cls. 18) supplements it where commerce-authorized legislation requires incidental means.

See also: **United States Code Title 1** (General Provisions), **Title 28** (Judiciary + Procedure), **Article II, §1** (Electoral College framework), and related USC Titles as referenced in `/usc_titles/README.md`.
