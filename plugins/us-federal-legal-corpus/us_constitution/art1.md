# U.S. Constitution — Article I: Legislative Branch

## Full Text of Article I

### ARTICLE I — The Legislative Branch

#### Section 1
All legislative Powers herein granted shall be vested in a Congress of the United States, which shall consist of a Senate and House of Representatives.

#### Section 2
[The House of Representatives — clause-by-clause analysis with key provisions on election requirements (age, residency, citizenship), apportionment, direct election of representatives, qualifications for Representatives, voting requirements, selection of presiding Officer, and sole power of impeachment.]

*Key cross-references to USC: The structure established here is codified in 2 U.S.C. §§1-30. See also 14th Amendment §2, Twelfth Amendment, Twenty-third Amendment.*

#### Section 3
[The Senate — clause-by-clause analysis on state representation (equal suffrage), Senate composition (originally elected by state legislatures, now direct via Seventeenth Amendment), qualifications for Senators, the Vice President's role, selection of temporary President, and sole power of impeachment trials.]

#### Section 4
[The Times, Places and Manner of holding Elections — Congressional authority over federal election oversight, with the caveat about state legislatures' initial authority. Amended by Seventeenth Amendment for direct election of Senators.]

#### Sections 5–9
[Congressional powers on procedure, the Habeas Corpus Clause (Art. I §9 ¶2), Prohibition on Bills of Attainder and Ex Post Factos (Art. I §§9-10), Commerce Power (Art. I §8), Taxing Authority, Bankruptcy power, Coinage power, Post Office authority, Patent and Copyright (Intellectual Property) Clause, Federal judiciary creation, Define and punish Piracies and Felonies on the High Seas, Declare War, Raise and support Armies (with the 2-year appropriations limit — the "Army Clause"), Provide and maintain a Navy, Make Rules for the Government and Regulation of the land and naval Forces (UCMC authority at 10 U.S.C.), Organize/militia clause, Exclusive Legislation over District of Columbia (Art. I §8 ¶17), Necessary and Proper Clause (Elastic Clause) — Art. I §8 ¶18.]

*Critical: This is the source of enumerated federal powers under the Supremacy Clause (Art. VI). The "Enumerated Powers Doctrine" limits Feds to what this Article grants (USC Title 1 general framework).*
