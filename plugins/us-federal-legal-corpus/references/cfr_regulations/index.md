# Code of Federal Regulations Titles and Agency Map

Sources: eCFR title API, current as of June 3, 2026, https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/titles.json; National Archives/OFR CFR overview, https://www.archives.gov/federal-register/cfr/.

The Code of Federal Regulations is divided into 50 subject-matter titles. Each title is divided into chapters that usually identify the issuing agency, office, board, commission, or governmentwide regulatory system. This index maps each CFR title to the principal agencies or regulatory systems commonly appearing in that title.

|---:|---|---|

## Currentness

The eCFR title API reported titles up to date as of June 3, 2026. Agency chapter assignments change as agencies are reorganized or rules are transferred, so use this as an orientation map and verify chapter-level authority in current eCFR text.
