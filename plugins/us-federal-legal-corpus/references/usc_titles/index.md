# United States Code Titles Index

Source: Office of the Law Revision Counsel, current United States Code download page, current through Public Law 119-95 (May 29, 2026): https://uscode.house.gov/download/download.shtml/.

The United States Code organizes the general and permanent laws of the United States by subject-matter title. Titles marked "positive law" by OLRC have been enacted by Congress as legal evidence of the law; non-positive-law titles remain prima facie evidence and should be checked against Statutes at Large when precision matters.

| Title | Subject | Summary |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | General Provisions | Rules for construing federal statutes, definitions, style, publication, and legal effect of federal laws and documents. |
| 2 | The Congress | Congressional organization, elections, officers, compensation, administration, legislative agencies, and congressional budget processes. |
| 3 | The President | Presidential elections, succession, compensation, executive residence, and related presidential and vice-presidential administration. |
| 4 | Flag and Seal, Seat of Government, and the States | The U.S. flag, Great Seal, District of Columbia seat-of-government provisions, and state-related legal status rules. |
| 5 | Government Organization and Employees | Federal agencies, administrative procedure, civil service, ethics, information policy, and federal employment systems. |
| 6 | Domestic Security | Homeland security organization, emergency preparedness, critical infrastructure, cybersecurity, and terrorism-related domestic security authorities. |
| 7 | Agriculture | Agriculture, commodity programs, food assistance, farm credit, marketing, conservation, and rural development statutes. |
| 8 | Aliens and Nationality | Immigration, nationality, naturalization, admission, removal, refugee/asylum, and related border and alien-registration laws. |
| 9 | Arbitration | Federal arbitration enforcement, maritime transactions, conventions, and arbitration award confirmation or vacatur. |
| 10 | Armed Forces | Organization, powers, personnel, procurement, discipline, and administration of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, and related defense components. |
| 11 | Bankruptcy | Federal bankruptcy jurisdiction, debtor-creditor estates, liquidation, reorganization, adjustment, and cross-border insolvency. |
| 12 | Banks and Banking | Banking charters, federal bank regulators, deposit insurance, monetary institutions, consumer finance agencies, and financial stability regimes. |
| 13 | Census | Census Bureau authorities, decennial census, data collection, confidentiality, surveys, and population statistics. |
| 14 | Coast Guard | Coast Guard establishment, missions, personnel, operations, administration, and maritime safety authorities. |
| 15 | Commerce and Trade | Business regulation, antitrust, securities, consumer protection, intellectual-property-adjacent commerce, standards, and trade programs. |
| 16 | Conservation | Natural resources, parks, forests, wildlife, water projects, public lands conservation, and environmental preservation programs. |
| 17 | Copyrights | Copyright subject matter, ownership, exclusive rights, infringement remedies, licensing, registration, and digital rights provisions. |
| 18 | Crimes and Criminal Procedure | Federal crimes, criminal procedure, imprisonment, sentencing, forfeiture, extradition, and law-enforcement powers. |
| 19 | Customs Duties | Customs administration, tariffs, import/export controls, trade remedies, and border revenue collection. |
| 20 | Education | Federal education programs, student aid, civil rights in education, school support, and education research/statistics. |
| 21 | Food and Drugs | Food, drugs, biologics, devices, cosmetics, controlled substances, public health safety, and FDA/DEA-related authorities. |
| 22 | Foreign Relations and Intercourse | State Department, diplomacy, foreign assistance, treaties, international organizations, sanctions, and consular affairs. |
| 23 | Highways | Federal-aid highways, surface transportation programs, highway safety, and transportation infrastructure funding. |
| 24 | Hospitals and Asylums | Legacy federal hospital, asylum, and soldiers' home provisions; many modern health programs appear in Title 42. |
| 25 | Indians | Indian tribes, Indian affairs administration, tribal lands, self-determination, economic development, and federal-tribal relations. |
| 26 | Internal Revenue Code | Federal taxes, tax procedure, exemptions, credits, penalties, information reporting, and IRS administration. |
| 27 | Intoxicating Liquors | Alcohol regulation, taxation-adjacent controls, interstate liquor shipment, and post-Prohibition federal liquor laws. |
| 28 | Judiciary and Judicial Procedure | Federal courts, judges, jurisdiction, venue, civil procedure, evidence-adjacent provisions, court administration, and Department of Justice. |
| 29 | Labor | Labor relations, wages and hours, workplace standards, employee protections, labor-management reporting, and employment programs. |
| 30 | Mineral Lands and Mining | Mining claims, mineral leasing, mine safety, coal, oil and gas resources, and related land-management authorities. |
| 31 | Money and Finance | Treasury, public debt, appropriations, financial management, claims, government accounting, and anti-money-laundering authorities. |
| 32 | National Guard | National Guard organization, personnel, training, activation, federal-state status, and administration. |
| 33 | Navigation and Navigable Waters | Rivers, harbors, navigation, flood control, maritime infrastructure, water pollution, and Coast Guard/Army Corps-related water authorities. |
| 34 | Crime Control and Law Enforcement | Criminal justice assistance, public safety grants, policing, victim services, sex-offender registration, and law-enforcement programs. |
| 35 | Patents | Patentability, patent prosecution, ownership, infringement, remedies, Patent Trial and Appeal Board proceedings, and USPTO patent administration. |
| 36 | Patriotic and National Observances, Ceremonies, and Organizations | National observances, patriotic customs, and federally chartered corporations and organizations. |
| 37 | Pay and Allowances of the Uniformed Services | Military and uniformed-service pay, allowances, bonuses, benefits, travel, and special compensation. |
| 38 | Veterans' Benefits | Veterans benefits, VA administration, disability compensation, pensions, education, health care, insurance, and memorial affairs. |
| 39 | Postal Service | U.S. Postal Service organization, operations, rates, accountability, employees, and postal regulatory framework. |
| 40 | Public Buildings, Property, and Works | Federal property, public buildings, GSA, construction, public works, and property disposal. |
| 41 | Public Contracts | Federal procurement policy, contract disputes, competition, service contracting, and public contract administration. |
| 42 | The Public Health and Welfare | Public health, Social Security, Medicare/Medicaid, civil rights, housing, environmental statutes, disaster relief, and broad welfare programs. |
| 43 | Public Lands | Public lands, surveys, land management, reclamation, minerals-adjacent public land law, and territorial land provisions. |
| 44 | Public Printing and Documents | Government publications, Federal Register, National Archives, public records, depository libraries, and information management. |
| 45 | Railroads | Railroad labor, safety, finance, passenger rail, rail reorganization, and rail employee benefits. |
| 46 | Shipping | Maritime commerce, vessel documentation, navigation, shipping, seamen, marine safety, and admiralty-adjacent statutory regimes. |
| 47 | Telecommunications | Communications, broadcasting, spectrum, common carriers, FCC authority, wire/radio communications, and telecommunications policy. |
| 48 | Territories and Insular Possessions | U.S. territories, insular areas, territorial governments, and related federal territorial administration. |
| 49 | Transportation | Transportation agencies, aviation, rail, motor carriers, pipelines, hazardous materials, transit, and transportation safety. |
| 50 | War and National Defense | National defense, intelligence, war powers, emergency authorities, defense production, and national security programs. |
| 51 | National and Commercial Space Programs | NASA, commercial space, space exploration, launch services, and national space policy programs. |
| 52 | Voting and Elections | Federal election law, voting rights, campaign finance, Help America Vote Act, and presidential/congressional election administration. |
| 53 | Reserved | Reserved title; no current enacted United States Code subject title. |
| 54 | National Park Service and Related Programs | National Park Service organic law, park system administration, historic preservation, and related national heritage programs. |

## Appendix Notes

OLRC lists appendices for some titles, including Titles 5, 11, 18, 28, and 50. Appendices may contain rules, transitional provisions, or related materials and are not necessarily enacted as part of the positive-law title.
