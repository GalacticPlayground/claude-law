# Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) — Title Index

The CFR is the codification of **general + permanent rules issued by federal executive agencies** under statutory authority from USC Titles. Each CFR title = a specific area of regulation administered by one or more agencies.

## Key CFR Titles for Legal Practice
| CFR Title | Agency/Subject Matter | Primary USC Authority |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------------------|
| 3 | Presidential Documents | Executive Orders; Proclamations (per USC §2) |
| **5** | **Agency Organization + Federal Employees** | **FOIA (5 U.S.C. §552); Hatch Act; GS classification/benefits** |
| **10** | **Nuclear Regulatory Commission** | Atomic Energy Act (42 USC §2011 et seq.) |
| **12** | **Banks and Banking** | FDIC/OCC/FRB regulations (USC Title 12) |
| **16** | **Trade Practices – FTC/CFPB** | **FTCA, Fair Credit Reporting Act regs; Truth in Lending (Reg Z)** |
| **17** | **Securities + Commodities** | SEC rules, Securities Exchange Act enforcement |
| **21** | **Food and Drug (FDA)** | FD&C Act delegation framework |
| **24** | **Housing + Urban Development (FHA/VA lending; equal housing = USC Title 42 §3601+)** |
| **26** | **Internal Revenue (IRS regulations on IRC, USC Title 27)** | IRC title text (§§1–9708+) |
| **28** | **Judiciary + Procedure** | Federal Rules of Civil/Criminal Practice; UCMJ rules |
| **29** | **Labor (OSHA / Wage-Hour / NLRB)** | FLSA, OSH Act, NLRA; union rules; whistleblower protection |
| **30** | **Mineral Resources (Mine Safety + extraction/leasing regs)** | MSHA + BLM mineral rights framework |

| **37** | **Patents, Trademarks, Copyrights: USPTO procedures/copyright office regulations** | USC Title 35 + Title 17 |

### Regulatory Framework Note
When analyzing a legal issue, check first what USC Title governs it, then what CFR Title carries the implementing rule. If a regulation exceeds its statutory scope (agency "ultra vires"}, see *Chevron* deference framework and the modern post-*Loper Bright* standard (*USC* §706).
