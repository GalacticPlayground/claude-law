# Montana Code Annotated — Court Rules Reference

## Court System Hierarchy

1. **State Supreme Court** (highest appellate authority)
2. **Court of Appeals** (intermediate appellate where applicable)
3. **Superior/District/Trial Court** (general jurisdiction trial level)
4. **Limited jurisdiction courts** (small claims, municipal, etc.)

## Key Rule Sets (statewide)

### Civil Procedure
- Rules of Civil Procedure (analogous to FRCP but for state court practice)
- Rules of Evidence (analogous to FRE)
- Rules of Appellate Procedure
- Local rules per county (filed in references/ below)

### Criminal Procedure
- Rules of Criminal Procedure (analogous to Fed. R. Crim. P.)
- Sentencing guidelines
- Pretrial procedure

### Specialty Courts
- Family court rules
- Probate court rules
- Small claims / limited jurisdiction
- Juvenile court rules

## Local Rules Per County
This corpus stores local rules as separate reference files, one per county or judicial district. County-level rules often include:
- Filing deadlines
- Courtroom procedures
- Local form requirements
- Standing orders from the presiding judge
