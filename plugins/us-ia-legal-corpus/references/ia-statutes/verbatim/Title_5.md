Order Legal Publications 
- Information about ways to obtain legal publications, including the Iowa Code.

Request Certified Copy of Iowa Code Provisions ($50 fee) 
- Iowa Code §2B.18 authorizes the Iowa Code Editor to certify Iowa Code provisions, for admission in a court of law or governmental office outside Iowa, by attesting to and authenticating how specific provisions appeared in the Iowa Code as of a certain date. Certification of Iowa Code provisions does not include researching Iowa law or providing legal advice. Most certifications are completed within one or two weeks.
