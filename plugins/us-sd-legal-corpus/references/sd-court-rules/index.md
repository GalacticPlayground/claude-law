## Common rule families referenced by consumer-debt / family-law / housing practice

- **Civil procedure** — pleading, motion practice, discovery, summary judgment, trial
- **Service of process** — who, how, when, default, jurisdictional limits
- **Discovery** — scope, limits, protective orders, sanctions, ESI
- **Default judgment** — entry, set-aside, execution
- **Small claims / limited-jurisdiction** — caps, procedures, appeal
- **Family law** — dissolution, parenting plan, child support worksheets, PFA
- **Landlord-tenant** — forcible entry and detainer, summary process, rent escrow
- **Probate / small estate** — informal probate, summary administration
- **Appellate procedure** — notice of appeal, record on appeal, briefing, oral argument
- **Evidence** — relevance, hearsay, expert, character, impeachment
- **Attorney conduct** — admission, discipline, pro hac vice, IOLTA, RPC (where adopted)
- **Judicial conduct** — recusal, ex parte communications
