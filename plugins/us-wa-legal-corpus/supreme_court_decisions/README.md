# Washington State Courts — Legal Reference Framework

## Jurisdiction Hierarchy

### WA Supreme Court (7 Justices) → Intermediate Appellate → Superior Courts → District Courts

Per **Article V §1** of the Washington Constitution and RCW 2.08:

```
Washington Supreme Court (supervisory jurisdiction over all state courts)
    ↓
Courts of Appeal (divided into 4 divisions per RCW 2.60)
    ↓
Superior Courts (trial level for civil/criminal/major jurisdiction — King County Superior is one of 39 superior courts statewide)
    ↓
District Courts + Municipal Courts (limited jurisdiction — minor civil disputes / misdemeanors / city ordinances)
```

## Washington General Court Rules ("GR") - Statewide Application to All Courts

The WA Supreme Court has rule-making authority per Art. V §4 that enables the **General Rules** framework:

| Rule | Subject | Scope of Application |
|------|---------|---------------------|
| GR 3 | Jurisdiction → civil/divorce/family/etc. case classification | Superior courts statewide |
| GR 6 | Pleadings/motions procedure rules (civil practice) | Superior courts statewide |
| GR 14 | General formatting/caption requirements; required content + page limits for all filings | All superior courts |
| GR 39 | Court access/transparency (e-filing rules framework in WA) | Statewide court administration |

### WA County-Specific Rules (per RCW Title 2.08 + Art. V §4)
- **King County**: KCSC Local Rules + General Orders (§§37.x per GR framework; local numbering system established with specific case classification rules). Also KCDC-specific rules per RCW Title 3.x (District Court jurisdiction).
- Other counties: Each has corresponding rules (e.g., Pierce County Superior Court rules, Snohomish County District Court rules). These exist within the statewide framework established by the WA Supreme Court (GR authority) but include venue-specific provisions where authorized.

## Washington's Court Rules Structure (Statewide + Venue-Specific)
1. **General Rules (GR)**: Statewide court rules; all procedural + jurisdictional framework
2. **Washington State Bar Association (WSBA)**: Ethics/discipline/professional conduct framework → RCW Title 2.xx + WA Superior Court Rules of Professional Conduct
3. **Local Superior Courts' Rules** per GR authority (each county has own Local Rules):  
   - King County Superior Court Local (KCSC-LR)  
   - Pierce County Superior Court Local (PCSC-LR)  
   Spokane County District etc. ...

## Venue: What's King County-Specific?
King County Superior Court (KCSC) and District Court (KCDC) are both within **the Fourth Judicial District** of Washington (RCW §2.08.150), which covers King, Snohomish, Island, San Juan counties collectively for certain administrative purposes.

However, each court's Local Rules govern:
- Filing schedules + specific deadlines unique to venues  
- Courtroom numbering / judges' assignments  
- Particular forms required by a county (e.g., family law child support worksheets per county-specific templates)
- Administrative Order procedures (how the presiding judge issues binding orders within that venue)
