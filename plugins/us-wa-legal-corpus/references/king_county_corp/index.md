# King County and Seattle Local Legal Corpus Index

This index points to the local court-rule and municipal-code corpora most likely to matter in King County, Washington practice. Confirm current rules and effective dates at the official source.

## King County Superior Court (KCSC) Local Rules

Official statewide local-rule portal: <https://www.courts.wa.gov/court_rules/?fa=court_rules.localsupbycrt&lsc=s17>

Official King County Superior Court local-rules pages are also published through King County Department of Judicial Administration pages.

Major KCSC rule families:

- Local General Rules (LGR)
- Local Civil Rules (LCR)
- Local Mandatory Arbitration Rules (LMAR)
- Local Family Law Rules (LFLR)
- Local Special Proceedings Rules (LSPR)
- Local Guardian ad Litem Rules (LGALR)
- Local Criminal Rules (LCrR)
- Local Juvenile Court Rules (LJuCR)
- Local Mental Proceedings Rules (LMPR)
- Local Court Management Rules (LCMR)
- Emergency local rules and standing orders, when posted

High-frequency KCSC civil and family-law lookup targets:

- LCR 4 - Civil case schedule and initial case management
- LCR 4.2 - Confirmation of joinder and issues in civil and family law cases
- LCR 7 - Civil motions
- LCR 26 - Discovery
- LCR 30 - Depositions
- LCR 37 - Discovery motions and sanctions
- LCR 40 - Assignment of cases and trial setting
- LCR 56 - Summary judgment
- LCR 59 - New trial and reconsideration workflows
- LMAR 1.1 through LMAR 8.6 - mandatory arbitration scope, transfer, hearing, award, and trial de novo
- LFLR 5 - Family law motions practice
- LFLR 6 - Parenting plans and child support
- LFLR 10 - Family law trial preparation
