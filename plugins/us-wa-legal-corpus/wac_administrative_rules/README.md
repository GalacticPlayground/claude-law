# Washington Administrative Code (WAC) — Corpus Reference

## Overview

Washington's WAC is the codification of regulations issued by state agencies under authority granted to them by the RCWs. Each Title of the WAC corresponds to a specific regulatory body/agency:

| WAC Title | Agency/Subject Matter | Notes for Practice |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------------|
| **WAC 10.x** | Dept. of Ecology / Environmental Regulations | Air emissions; water rights (RCW §90); pollution control standards |
| **WAC 16.x** | Dept. of Agriculture (Washington) | Pesticide/fertilizer use; livestock inspection + food safety requirements; plant health codes |
| WAC 18-50+ | Various Professional & Occupational Boards | Contractor license board (RCW §18.27), cosmetology/healthcare/professional licensing rules |
| **WAC 391** | Higher Education (Board of Governors) | Community college requirements; state university administration policies |
| WAC 246 | Dept. of Health | Medical practice board; public health; medical licensing + nursing home standards |
| **WAC 296** | L&I / Worker's Comp regulations | **WA workers comp claims/employer obligations; safety codes (OSHA-WA equivalent)** |
| WAC 388 | Dept. of Social + Health Services | TANF/SNAP/welfare administration; DSHS public assistance standards |
| **WAC 192** | Department of Licensing (DOL) | Vehicle registration/license/insurance requirements; boating; firearms rules for WA |
| WAC 480+ | Utilities (WA PUC + Energy/Occupancy rules) | Public utility commission regulations including gas/electric regulation and consumer rate-setting standards |

## Regulatory Relationship to RCWs
Each WAC Title has an underlying authorizing statute in the RCW. When analyzing a legal issue:  
1. Identify the subject matter → map it to the WAC Title for that regulatory agency's authority  
2. Trace backward to the enabling RCW chapter(s) to understand scope of delegation + limits on rulemaking
3. Where the WAC exceeds its RCW-authority, cite **Chadwick v. Department of Licensing**.
