# King County Legal Corpus — Comprehensive Reference Library

## Jurisdictional Authority

King County operates under authority granted by:  
1. **Washington State Constitution Art. XI** (Local Government provisions)  
2. **RCW Title 35+** (Statutory enabling framework for counties/cities in WA)  
3. **Individual city charters & municipal codes** (where authorized by statute/Art. XI §4)

### Current Court Structure:
- **King County Superior Court (KCSC)** (Superior Division - Fourth Judicial District) → Handles felony cases, major civil matters ($100K+), family law, probate  
- **King County District Court (KCDC)** (District Court for Fourth Judicial District) → Handles misdemeanors, limited jurisdiction civil (under $100K), small claims  

Both courts operate under:
- WA Supreme Court General Rules (GR framework statewide)  
- Venue-specific local rules + administrative orders per county  
- King County specific filing/processing requirements per the court's own standing orders

## Cross-Jurisdiction Notes for Legal Practice in King County
When practicing law in King County, always consider which layer controls:
1. **Federal Law** (when applicable — e.g., federal bankruptcy, immigration → USC/CFR corpus)  
2. **Washington State Law** → RCWs (statutes) + WACs (agency rules + administrative regulations); WA Supreme Court precedents  
3. **King County Ordinances** → county-level legislation per Art. XI framework  
4. **City/Municipal Codes** → specific to each incorporated jurisdiction within the county  
5. **Federal Court Rules** → for cases filed in U.S. District Court (Western District of Washington — King County sits within WDW)
