New Mexico Compilation Commission – Official Legal Publisher of State of New Mexico 

 Official Legal Publisher 

 of State of New Mexico

 Pursuant to Chapter 12, Article 1 of the New Mexico Statutes Annotated 1978 , the New Mexico Compilation Commission serves the vital role of publishing and distributing the official laws of the State of New Mexico to its citizens.
